Quarter 1

A small village called Sitio Bebe is facing a health crisis due to its lack of a natural canal, causing frequent droughts. The local authorities are importing water and relying on wells for clean water. As Filipinos, we must help ease the suffering of the community, as the government has already spent money on pandemic response.

Quarter two

At Blooming Wellness Hub, we believe that true wellness blooms from within. Our channel is dedicated to inspiring and guiding you on your journey to a healthier lifestyle. Through engaging discussions, insightful podcasts, and advice, we aim to empower you to take charge of your physical, mental, social, and spiritual health.

Quarter 3
